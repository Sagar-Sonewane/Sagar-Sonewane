### 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a full-stack developer and **MCA student at GHRCEM, Nagpur**, focused on building end-to-end digital products from first principles. I love working at the intersection of **clean systems architecture, realtime collaboration, and craft-driven UI engineering**. 

I integrate AI tooling into my development workflows to accelerate prototyping, while strictly validating every line of code, architecture decision, and user experience by hand.
