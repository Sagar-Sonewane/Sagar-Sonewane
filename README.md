### 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a passionate **Full-Stack Developer** and **MCA student at G H Raisoni College of Engineering and Management (GHRCEM), Nagpur** (Class of 2026). My work centers on building scalable, realtime web applications, robust distributed state architectures, and craft-driven interactive user experiences.

I bridge product intuition with systems engineering: from low-latency WebRTC audio rooms and CRDT-synced collaborative canvases, to speech-recognition assessment engines and community rental platforms.
